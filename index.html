The garage is located at Cleveland St, Wirral, Merseyside, CH41 4JN, near Wirral Town Centre. We provide convenient parking facilities for customers, including wheelchair-accessible parking.
What services are available?
S.CAR REPAIR CENTRE provides professional car servicing, MOTs, diagnostics, bodywork, paint repairs, and used car sales. Our experienced technicians handle a wide range of vehicle maintenance needs for all makes and models.

Call us directly at 07359793570 to book an appointment or visit the garage directly. Our team is ready to assist with your vehicle service requirements during our opening hours.
